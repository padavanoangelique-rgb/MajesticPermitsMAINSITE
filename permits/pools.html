S-101

Pools

What this filing usually needs

  • A site plan with the water, the deck, and the barrier.
  • The pool equipment location and the electrical for the pump and the light.
  • The screen enclosure, if there is one, called out instead of assumed.
  • Engineering for the shell and the surcharge on the existing house when the city requires it.

The city can ask for more once it sees the address. We use that city's form. We do not rename another city's packet.
