A-101

Impact windows

What this filing usually needs

  • The city's window application and an opening schedule. Every window gets an ID, a size, and a location.
  • A current product approval for that exact series. In Miami-Dade and Broward it has to be approved for the high-velocity zone.
  • The design pressure the house needs and the design pressure the product provides.
  • A floor plan that marks bedrooms, because a bedroom window still has to meet the egress sizes.
  • A Notice of Commencement when the contract is over the amount that county requires.

The city can ask for more once it sees the address. We use that city's form. We do not rename another city's packet.
