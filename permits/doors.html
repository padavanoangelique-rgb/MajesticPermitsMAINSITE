A-102

Doors

What this filing usually needs

  • The door on its own line. It is not filed off the window approval next to it.
  • The product approval for that door, including the hardware that was tested with it.
  • The rough opening size. A door that is wider than the opening it replaces is a different review.
  • A floor plan that shows which opening it is.
  • Egress and the exit path if the door is the required exit from a bedroom or the house.

The city can ask for more once it sees the address. We use that city's form. We do not rename another city's packet.
