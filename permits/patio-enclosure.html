S-201

Patio enclosure

What this filing usually needs

  • A site plan and elevations showing the enclosure, the slab, and how it meets the house.
  • The product approval for that screen or aluminum system, or engineering sealed for that cage.
  • The connection to the existing structure. A freestanding cage and one tied into the fascia are not filed the same way.
  • If a pool sits inside the enclosure, say so. A new pool is its own permit. We do not hide it inside the cage.
  • Wind exposure for that address. Miami-Dade and Broward are the high-velocity zone. Palm Beach still has a design pressure.

The city can ask for more once it sees the address. We use that city's form. We do not rename another city's packet.
