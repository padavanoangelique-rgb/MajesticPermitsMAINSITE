A-501

Fencing

What this filing usually needs

  • A site plan that shows the property lines, the fence line, the height, and every gate.
  • The material. A wood privacy fence, an aluminum picket, and a masonry wall are not the same packet.
  • If the fence is the barrier for a pool, the height, the latch, and which way the gate swings have to match the pool rules.
  • Taller fences and masonry in Miami-Dade and Broward often need a wind design. We do not file a wall as if it were a picket fence.
  • An association letter is separate from the city permit. We ask which one applies before we tell you the city is the only stop.

The city can ask for more once it sees the address. We use that city's form. We do not rename another city's packet.
