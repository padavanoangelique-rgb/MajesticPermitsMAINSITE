P-101

Plumbing

What this filing usually needs

  • The plumbing application and what is being replaced.
  • A water heater in the same location is not the same permit as a repipe.
  • Fixture moves belong on the bath or kitchen plan, not on a blank plumbing form.

The city can ask for more once it sees the address. We use that city's form. We do not rename another city's packet.
