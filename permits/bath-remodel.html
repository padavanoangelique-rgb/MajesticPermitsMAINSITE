A-401

Bath remodel

What this filing usually needs

  • An existing floor plan and a proposed plan, with the fixtures that stay and the ones that move.
  • A plumbing layout if a toilet, shower, or sink changes location. A faucet swapped in the same spot is often not this permit.
  • Electrical if new lights, a fan, a heated floor, or a new circuit is part of the job.
  • If a bathroom window is replaced, that opening needs its own product approval. It is not covered by the bath drawing.
  • A Notice of Commencement when the contract is over the amount that county requires.

The city can ask for more once it sees the address. We use that city's form. We do not rename another city's packet.
