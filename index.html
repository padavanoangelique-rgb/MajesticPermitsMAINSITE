Questions

Permits,
explained.

What does a permit expediter do?

A permit expediter prepares and submits your building permit application to the city or county, tracks the review, responds to corrections and requests for information, and follows up until it's approved — so you don't have to navigate the process yourself.

How long does it take to get a building permit in Miami-Dade, Broward, or Palm Beach County?

Timelines vary by jurisdiction, permit type, and current review volume — a straightforward window or door permit often moves faster than a roofing or renovation permit with multiple trades. Your portal shows the current stage and estimated timing for your specific application.

Do I need a permit for impact windows or doors in Florida?

Yes. Impact window and door installations in Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties require a building permit, plus product approval (NOA) documentation for the specific windows or doors being installed.

What areas does Majestic Permits serve?

Miami-Dade County, Broward County, and Palm Beach County — for windows, doors, roofing, pools and screen enclosures, electrical, HVAC, plumbing, and renovation projects.

How much does permit expediting cost?

A flat, upfront fee per permit for single projects, or a monthly retainer for contractors with ongoing volume. Contact us for a quote specific to your project.
