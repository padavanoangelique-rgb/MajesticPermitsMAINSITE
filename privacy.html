Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Majestic Construction Permits LLC, doing business as Majestic Permits and The Permit Closer (“we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, our client hub at hub.majesticpermits.com, or our permit expediting services across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach Counties.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Property and project information — site address, permit type, jurisdiction, and related project details
  • Account information for contractors and homeowners using our client hub
  • Payment information processed through our payment provider (Stripe) — we do not store your full card number on our own servers
  • Communications you send us, including messages, emails, and support requests

How We Use Your Information

  • To prepare, submit, and track building permit applications on your behalf
  • To communicate with you about the status of your project
  • To process payments for our services
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support
  • To improve our services and website

Text Message (SMS) Communications

With your consent, we send text message updates about your permit or project — for example, when an inspection is requested or scheduled. By providing your phone number and opting in, you agree to receive these messages.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your project activity. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages, or HELP for assistance. Opting out of text messages will not affect your ability to receive service by phone, email, or through the client hub.

Email Communications

We send project status updates and account-related emails through our email service provider. You may unsubscribe from non-essential emails at any time using the link provided in those emails.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide our services:

  • With the relevant city or county building department, as required to file and track your permit
  • With service providers who help us operate — including payment processing, text messaging, email delivery, and secure data storage
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted data storage and secure connections, to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain project and account information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, accounting, or recordkeeping requirements.

Your Choices

  • You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP
  • You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in those emails
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us below

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults and are not directed to individuals under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent changes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at angelique@majesticpermits.com.
