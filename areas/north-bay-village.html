Miami-Dade County

North Bay Village

North Bay Village is its own building department in Miami-Dade County. We file that city's application, opening schedule, product approvals, and floor plan. We do not take another city's form and change the name.

Miami-Dade and Broward are in the high-velocity hurricane zone. The window or door needs a current Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance, or a Florida Product Approval approved for that zone.

The city comes from the first two digits of the 13-digit folio.
