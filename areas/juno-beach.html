Juno Beach

Juno Beach is its own building department in Palm Beach County. We file that city's application, opening schedule, product approvals, and floor plan. We do not take another city's form and change the name.

Palm Beach is a wind-borne debris region, not the high-velocity zone. The product still needs a current Florida Product Approval or a Notice of Acceptance, and the design pressure has to match that house.

The city comes from the first two digits of the 17-digit parcel control number.
